## 1. MARKET SIZE & TIMING

### The Opportunity
- **US legal cannabis market:** $31.5B in 2025 (BDSA), projected $30.5B in 2026 (Whitney Economics)
- **45,000+** licensed cannabis operators in the US
- **~500** cannabis-specialized CPA firms
- **TAM:** ~$540M/year (45K operators × $1,000/mo avg)
- **SAM:** ~$189M/year (operators needing dedicated software)
- **SOM (Year 1):** ~$1M ARR (120 customers at $700/mo blended)

### The Timing Problem
- **280E rescheduling to Schedule III** is actively happening (Trump signed executive order Dec 2025)
- If 280E is fully repealed, the core problem disappears
- **BUT:** Rescheduling ≠ repeal. Even under Schedule III, cannabis businesses will face:
  - Stricter federal oversight
  - New compliance requirements
  - DEA registration requirements
  - IRC 471 inventory accounting still applies
  - State-level taxes persist
- **Window:** 12-24 months of peak pain before rescheduling fully takes effect

### The Counter-Argument (Why It Still Works)
Even if 280E is repealed:
1. Cannabis accounting remains uniquely complex (state taxes, multi-entity, Metrc)
2. Banking/financial operations become MORE important as industry legitimizes
3. M&A activity increases → need for audit-ready financials
4. The platform's value prop broadens from "280E compliance" to "financial operations"
5. Switching costs create lock-in
