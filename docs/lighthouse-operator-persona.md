# Lighthouse Operator Persona

## Target Profile

**Name:** Westside Apothecary (fictional composite of ideal first customer)

**Type:** Single-state, single-location dispensary with vertical integration aspirations

**Revenue:** $3.5M annually

**Location:** Oakland, CA (Metrc state, tax-aggressive environment)

**Owner:** Maya Rodriguez, age 38, MBA, former finance director at a mid-tier MSO

### Business Characteristics

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Entity structure | Single LLC (currently), exploring second entity for cultivation |
| Licenses | Type 10 (retail), applying for Type 2C (cultivation — small) |
| Employees | 12 W-2, 8 1099 contract (budtenders, delivery) |
| COGS breakdown | 55% procured inventory, 25% in-house cultivation (small), 20% overhead |
| Tax situation | Already doing 280E with current CPA, feels underserved |
| Tech stack | Metrc, QuickBooks Online, Excel-heavy reporting |

### Psychographics

- **Analytical mindset** — understands numbers, comfortable with spreadsheets
- **Cannabis-native** — 8+ years in industry, seen multiple compliance cycles
- **Growth-oriented** — wants to open 2 more locations in next 24 months
- **Risk-aware** — fears IRS audit, wants "bulletproof" documentation
- **Time-constrained** — wears multiple hats, needs tools that save time

### Pain Points

1. **Current CPA only does 280E "checklist"** — no proactive allocation strategy; they just deduct what's allowed and move on
2. **No visibility into 471(c) election** — heard about it but doesn't know how to quantify the benefit
3. **Metrc data not integrated** — manually exports packages to CSV, then re-formats in Excel
4. **Audit dread** — worried about "reasonable" determinations; wants robust audit trail
5. **Scaling anxiety** — knows current manual process breaks at 2+ locations

### Why They're the Perfect First Customer

1. **High engagement propensity** — Maya is analytically-minded and will use the product daily
2. **Clear success metrics** — we can measure: time saved, tax savings quantified, audit readiness
3. **Strategic expansion potential** — her cultivation license application means she'll need multi-entity features within 6 months
4. **Reference quality** — articulate, tech-comfortable, networked in CA operator community
5. **Geographic strategic** — California is the largest market; a CA success story carries weight

### Onboarding Journey

**Pre-launch (Week -1 to 0)**
- Intro via mutual founder connection (warm)
- Demo call focused on her specific COGS allocation challenge
- Agree to 90-day founding customer trial ($1/mo, 50% off thereafter if refer another)
- Sign NDA + feedback agreement

**Week 1 — Metrc Integration**
- Connect her Metrc vendor integrator key (she already has one)
- Pull 6 months of packages → auto-categorize into our 280E buckets
- Review allocation percentages with her CPA on a shared call

**Week 2 — 471(c) Election Strategy**
- Show the 471(c) calculator specific to her revenue mix
- Model: staying at 65% reclassifiable vs moving to 85% with entity split
- She consults her CPA; they jointly run numbers

**Week 3 — First Export**
- Generate the "280E Support Schedule" for 2024 tax year
- Export to both PDF and Excel
- Her CPA reviews and provides feedback (we iterate on format)

**Week 4 — Early Product Feedback**
- Deep-dive session: what's missing? What's broken?
- Prioritization workshop: choose 1–2 features we'll build next month
- Agree on case study format (we document her story)

### Expected Outcomes

**Quantitative:**
- $45K–$65K additional tax deduction quantified via 471(c) optimization
- 10+ hours/month saved on manual reporting
- Audit defensibility score increases from " nervy " to " confident "

**Qualitative:**
- Testimonial quote: "Tranquillo finally makes 280E _defensible_ rather than just deductible."
- Permission to name-drop in outreach to 3 other operators
- Referral to her CPA (who then has operator clients asking)

### Red Flags to Avoid

- If she needs inventory management or POS features — **not our core**
- If she expects us to replace her CPA — **we complement the CPA, not replace**
- If she's not willing to share feedback weekly — **no time investment means no partnership**

## Alternative Persona: CPA Firm Partner

**Firm:** GreenLedger CPAs (fictional composite, $5M revenue firm)

**Location:** Denver, CO; satellite office in Los Angeles

**Partners:** 3 partners, 12 staff accountants

**Client portfolio:** 35 cannabis clients; average revenue $4M; mix of retail (60%), cultivation (25%), manufacturer (15%)

**Current workflow:**
1. Client sends QuickBooks export + Metrc CSV annually in March
2. Staff manually reconciles 3 months of data to compute 280E deduction
3. Partner reviews and signs off; no ongoing allocation tracking
4. Clients receive static PDF report; no interactive component

**Why They Could Be First:**
- Existing client base = immediate pipeline
- Their endorsement carries weight in cannabis accounting circles
- Their workflow pain is exactly what our audit trail + automation solves
- Can onboard 3–5 clients within first month (fast validation)

**Why They Might Be Second:**
- Our CPA-specific features (white-label, bulk client mgmt, firm dashboard) are not fully built yet
- Requires more hand-holding than anoperator who uses product directly
- Sales cycle is longer (firm-level decision vs individual owner)

## Final Selection Criteria

When evaluating candidates, score them on:

1. **Engagement willingness** (weight: 30%)
   - Willing to give weekly feedback?
   - Available for calls?
   - Willing to be case study?

2. **Product-market fit** (weight: 25%)
   - Core use case matches?
   - Revenue range appropriate?
   - License types we support?

3. **Reference quality** (weight: 20%)
   - Articulate?
   - Networked?
   - Respected in industry?

4. **Expansion potential** (weight: 15%)
   - Will grow into more features (e.g., entity structuring, multi-entity)?
   - Likely to refer others?

5. **Low red-flag count** (weight: 10%)
   - No scope-creep demands
   - No timeline pressure that exceeds our capacity

**Selection threshold:** Average score ≥ 3.5 / 5.0 across these criteria.
