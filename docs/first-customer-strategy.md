# First Customer Strategy — Tranquillo Green

## Executive Summary

We are at **launch readiness 85%**. The product is functional but needs refinement before customer exposure. The critical question: **do we onboard a lighthouse operator or a CPA firm first?**

**Recommendation: Start with a lighthouse dispensary operator, then immediately follow with a CPA firm partner.**

---

## Option A: Lighthouse Dispensary Operator

### Profile
- Single retail location or 2–3 vertically integrated licenses
- Annual revenue $2M — $10M
- Tax-aware founder/CFO (already thinking about 280E)
- Located in CA, CO, OR, or WA (Metrc states)
- Willing to provide weekly feedback and co-develop features

### Why Operator First
1. **Product-fit validation** — Our 280E + 471(c) engine + allocation dashboard is built for operators. They will use the core product daily.
2. **Real-world stress test** — Only operators will find edge cases in transaction categorization, COGS allocation, and audit trail generation.
3. **Faster time-to-value** — Operators see immediate benefit: defensible tax positions, audit-ready reports, Metrc integration hooks.
4. **Case study potential** — Success story from one operator can be repurposed as marketing for broader launch.

### Risks
- Single-customer feedback may not represent MSO needs
- If operator's needs diverge too far from roadmap, scope creep risk
- Operator expectations may exceed current automation level

### Onboarding Cadence
- Week 1: Metrc connection + initial transaction import
- Week 2: 471(c) election guidance + allocation review
- Week 3: First export package generation (CPA-ready)
- Week 4: Feedback collection + roadmap prioritization

### Success Criteria
- Daily active usage for 14+ consecutive days
- At least 50 transactions processed through the platform
- One export package generated and reviewed by their CPA
- Written testimonial and willingness to be reference customer

---

## Option B: CPA Firm Partner

### Profile
- Mid-size cannabis-specialized CPA firm (10–50 clients)
- Already preparing 280E / 471(c) work for clients
- Tech-forward (already uses tax tech/automation)
- Open to white-labeled client portal
- Can commit to quarterly compliance reviews

### Why CPA First
1. **Multiplicative reach** — One CPA can onboard 10+ clients simultaneously.
2. **Higher LTV** — CPA plan $199/mo vs Essentials $299/mo; but 10 clients = $1990/mo potential.
3. **Strategic defensibility** — CPA endorsement is the strongest signal for the broader market.
4. **Revenue recognition** — CPA firms pay on time, budget for tools annually.

### Risks
- Our product is currently operator-first; CPA needs white-label reports, client management UI built
- CPA workflow tools still immature (client provisioning, bulk operations)
- Risk of being perceived as "CPA-only" not "operator-first"
- Longer sales cycle (firm-level procurement)

### Onboarding Cadence
- Week 1: Firm admin setup + first client company created
- Week 2: Client onboarding workflow + report generation
- Week 3: Multi-client dashboard demo
- Week 4: Integration into firm's service delivery process

### Success Criteria
- At least 3 client companies onboarded within first month
- Firm uses our export packages in 2+ client filings
- Positive feedback from firm partners on audit defensibility
- Referral to at least one additional firm

## Decision Matrix

| Criterion | Operator (A) | CPA Firm (B) |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Time-to-value | 2 weeks | 4+ weeks |
| Multiplier effect | 1x | 10–20x clients |
| Product readiness match | High (core UI complete) | Medium (CPA tools partial) |
| Revenue per customer | $299/mo | $199/mo (but more clients) |
| Strategic defensibility | Moderate | Strong |
| Feedback type | Operational UX | Workflow integration |
| Risk profile | Low | Medium-High |

---

## Recommended Path: Hybrid Sequential

**Phase 1 — Lighthouse Operator (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Identify 3–5 candidate operators from network/outreach
2. Prioritize: CA dispensary, single location, tech-savvy founder
3. Onboard first operator with white-glove support (direct founder involvement)
4. Document feedback; ship fixes weekly based on their usage

**Phase 2 — CPA Firm (Weeks 5–8)**
1. Based on operator learnings, complete CPA firm features (client provisioning, white-label branding)
2. Approach 2–3 already-engaged firms (via founder network)
3. Offer 6-month pilot at no charge in exchange for structured feedback
4. Enable them to onboard 3–5 of their clients using operator experience as reference

**Phase 3 — Scale (Weeks 9–12)**
1. Case study from operator + testimonial from CPA
2. Launch waitlist with "Join existing customers" social proof
3. Begin outbound to 50 target operators (dispensaries $2–10M)
4. Begin outbound to 20 CPA firms (cannabis-specialized)
