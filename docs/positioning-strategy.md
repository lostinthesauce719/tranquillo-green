# Tranquillo Green — Positioning Strategy

## Category
**Cannabis Financial Operations Platform** — the financial operating layer between POS and CPA.

Not "accounting software" (too generic). Not "compliance software" (too narrow). Not "ERP" (too broad).

## UVP (One-Line)
> "The only accounting platform that automates 280E and keeps you audit-ready."

## Emotional Variant
> "Stop fearing the IRS. Cannabis accounting that makes 280E automatic."

## Tagline
> **"Audit-ready from day one."**

Alternatives:
- "280E solved. Sleep restored."
- "Cannabis books, finally clean."

## "Only We" Statement
> "Only Tranquillo automatically allocates COGS under 280E, syncs Metrc inventory in real time, and exports CPA-ready tax workpapers — all without leaving QuickBooks."

## 2x2 Competitive Matrix

| | **Generic** | **Cannabis-Specific** |
|---|---|---|
| **Accounting Depth** | QuickBooks, Xero, Sage | **TRANQUILLO GREEN** (empty quadrant) |
| **Compliance/POS Focus** | N/A | Dutchie, Treez, Akerna (collapsed) |

**Empty quadrant:** Nobody combines deep accounting + cannabis-specific features. Tranquillo is the only player there.

## Competitor Weaknesses

### 1. QuickBooks — Ticking Time Bomb
- No Metrc integration
- TOS allows termination of cannabis accounts
- Breaks at $5-10M revenue
- No 280E awareness whatsoever

### 2. POS-First Platforms (Dutchie, Treez, Greenbits)
- Treat accounting as afterthought
- Ignore the pain that actually kills operators (70%+ effective tax rates)
- No COGS allocation engine

### 3. Akerna / MJ Freeway — Collapsed
- Sold for $5M, merged with crypto company
- Orphaned customer base = switching-ready prospects

## First Buyer
**Cannabis-specialized CPA firms** (not operators).
- Single CPA firm brings 10-40 operator accounts
- Target: 2-15 person firms with 5-40 cannabis clients
- They feel the 280E pain most acutely

## Beachhead Market
**Colorado** single-license dispensaries.
- Mature market, Metrc-mandatory
- Dense CPA ecosystem
- Deep 280E pain understanding
- NOT Oklahoma (shakeout, razor-thin margins)

## Launch Offer
**"280E Recovery Audit"** — free 48-hour audit of last 2 years of QuickBooks data showing dollar savings from proper COGS allocation. Proves value before asking for money.

## Aha Moment
Metrc + QuickBooks connected → 280E allocation report in **3 minutes** vs. **8-20 hours** of manual CPA work. Show dollar delta.

## Positioning Traps (What NOT to Say)
1. ❌ "We replace QuickBooks" (massive switching costs)
2. ❌ "We're a seed-to-sale platform" (that's Metrc's job)
3. ❌ "Built for cannabis" (table stakes, not differentiator)
4. ❌ "We help you save money" (vague, unprovable)
5. ❌ "All-in-one solution" (dilutes into ERP territory)

## 90-Day Targets
- 5-8 CPA firms active
- 25-40 operator accounts connected
- 15-20 audits completed
- 10+ QuickBooks journal entry exports

### CPAs

### Investors
