# Tranquillo Green — GTM Launch Playbook

## Launch Offer: "280E Recovery Audit"
Free 48-hour audit of last 2 years of QuickBooks data showing dollar savings from proper COGS allocation. Proves value before asking for money.

## Target Market
**First buyers:** Cannabis-specialized CPA firms (not operators)
- Single CPA firm brings 10-40 operator accounts
- Target: 2-15 person firms with 5-40 cannabis clients

**Beachhead:** Colorado single-license dispensaries
- Mature market, Metrc-mandatory
- Dense CPA ecosystem
- Deep 280E pain understanding

## Channel Strategy
1. Direct outreach to CO CPA firms (LinkedIn, referrals)
2. "280E Recovery Audit" as lead magnet
3. CPA firms bring their operator clients
4. Operators see the value through CPA recommendation

## Pricing Tiers (Draft)
- **Free:** 280E Recovery Audit (lead gen)
- **Solo:** $X/mo — single operator, basic allocation
- **CPA:** $X/mo — multi-client dashboard, export packets
- **Enterprise:** Custom — multi-state, API access

## 90-Day Milestones
- 5-8 CPA firms active
- 25-40 operator accounts connected
- 15-20 audits completed
- 10+ QuickBooks journal entry exports

## Key Metrics
- Audit conversion rate (audit → paid)
- Time-to-value (connect Metrc → first allocation report)
- CPA firm expansion rate (clients per firm)
- Monthly recurring revenue

## Competitive Intel
- **QuickBooks:** No 280E awareness, TOS risk for cannabis, breaks at scale
- **Dutchie/Treez:** POS-first, accounting is afterthought
- **Akerna/MJ Freeway:** Collapsed ($5M sale), orphaned customers = prospects
- **365 Cannabis:** ERP, too broad, too expensive for small operators
