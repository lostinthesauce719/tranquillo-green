## Demo Script (30-minute call)

### Minute 0–5: Context Setting
> "Thanks for your time. We're Tranquillo Green. We help cannabis operators like you build an audit-defensible 280E position without the spreadsheet nightmare. We do this by connecting directly to Metrc, applying the 471(c) inventory capitalization rules, and producing the exact reports IRS auditors want to see."

### Minute 5–10: Their Current Process
> "Before I show you anything, tell me about your current 280E workflow. How do you track COGS? Who prepares the tax return? What's the biggest headache?"

*(Take notes. Mirror their language.)*

### Minute 10–20: Live Demo (focused on THEIR pain)
- If they say **"manually export Metrc"** → show Metrc auto-import
- If they say **"unsure what's deductible"** → show allocation engine with categorical breakdown
- If they say **"afraid of audit"** → show audit trail + support schedule generation
- If they say **"CPA asks for weird reports"** → show custom export templates

### Minute 20–25: Pilot Offer
> "We're looking for 3 founding operators to work with us for 90 days. You'd get:
> - Full platform access for $1/mo
> - Weekly check-ins with our team
> - Direct influence on roadmap
> - Lifetime 50% discount after pilot
>
> In exchange: weekly feedback, willingness to be a reference case study."

### Minute 25–28: Objection Handling (prepare responses)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I don't have time" | "That's why we built this — to save you 10+ hours/month. Pilot commitment is just 30 min/week." |
| "My CPA says we're fine" | "Great! This just makes it bulletproof. More defensibility = lower audit risk." |
| "We're building our own tool" | "That's smart. We can show you what good looks like — if you build, ours is a solid benchmark." |
| "What's the catch?" | "No catch. We need real-world operators to stress-test before we launch widely." |
| "We're too small/large" | "We're targeting $2–10M revenue. If you're outside that, happy to refer you to a partner." |

### Minute 28–30: Next Steps
> "If this sounds interesting, next step is a 30-day pilot at $1. No commitment to continue. We'll set you up, you use it with your real data, and we meet weekly to iterate. Deal?"

## Red Flag Detection (stop pursuit if)

- They want custom features that don't align with roadmap (e.g., "build me an POS")
- They're not using Metrc (too much manual work for us)
- They can't commit to weekly feedback (no engagement)
- They're already working with a direct competitor
- They expect us to replace their CPA (we complement, not replace)
