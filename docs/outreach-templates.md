## Qualification Script (phone screen, 5 minutes)

> "Before we do a full demo, I have a few quick questions to make sure this is a fit:
>
> 1. Are you actively using Metrc daily? (If no — we require Metrc)
> 2. Do you have a CPA who handles your tax return? (If no — we're a tool, not a replacement)
> 3. What's your annual revenue range? (We target $2–10M)
> 4. Are you willing to try something new and give weekly feedback for 90 days? (If no — not a pilot candidate)
>
> If those all check out, let's schedule a 20-minute demo."

## Common Objection Responses

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We're too small" | "Our sweet spot is $2–10M. Sounds like you're at the lower end — still worth a look if you're using Metrc." |
| "We're too big" | "We support multi-entity, but our current focus is single/double-entity operators. Might not be ready for MSO yet." |
| "We already have something" | "What are you using? Happy to show you a comparison — if ours isn't better, no hard feelings." |
| "No time" | "That's exactly why we built this — to save you 10+ hours/month on tax prep. Pilot is only $1 and 30 min/week of your time." |
| "My CPA handles it" | "That's great — we make your CPA's job easier. They get cleaner data and bulletproof documentation." |
| "Don't want to be a guinea pig" | "Fair — you'd be a founding operator, not a guinea pig. You get lifetime 50% off for early feedback." |
| "Not now, maybe later" | "Understood. Mind if I circle back in 3 months? We'll be launching publicly by then anyway." |
