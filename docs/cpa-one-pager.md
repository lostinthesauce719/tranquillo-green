## The Problem

- **Section 280E** forces cannabis operators to pay taxes on gross profit, not net — disallowing standard business deductions
- **Average effective tax rate: 70%+** without proper COGS allocation methodology
- **CPAs rebuild allocations from scratch** for every client — consuming 8–20 hours per return

## The Solution

- **280E + 471(c) Dual-Path Allocation Engine** — automatic COGS allocation with a defensible, audit-ready methodology
- **471(c) Small Business Election** — captures facility rent, inventory labor, and shipping into deductible COGS categories
- **Metrc Integration** — real-time sync between state seed-to-sale tracking and your client's chart of accounts
- **CPA Export Packets** — one-click QuickBooks Online export with audit-ready workpapers — no spreadsheet mess
